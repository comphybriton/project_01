Our story

It started with a simple no.

We refused to choose between gentle and effective. So we made a deodorant for sensitive skin that actually works — and smells good doing it.

The beginning

Every natural one let us down.

It began in a bathroom, with a drawer full of half-used tubes. Every natural deodorant either quit by lunchtime or left skin red and irritated — usually thanks to bicarb. Every one that actually worked was full of aluminium and things we couldn't pronounce.

"Gentle and effective" wasn't a product we could buy. So we spent two years making it — swapping bicarb for magnesium and testing balm after balm until it was kind to sensitive skin, smelled great, and lasted the whole day.

"You shouldn't have to choose. So we made sure you don't."

What we stand for

Trusted routines, positive habits.

No compromise
Gentle on sensitive skin, tough on odour. If it doesn't work, it doesn't ship.
Nothing wasted
Recyclable packaging and a brass key that gets every last bit. Sustainability by design.
Kept simple
Honest ingredients, plain language. We're big on simplicity.
From the founder

I started AGAIN because I wanted a morning ritual I could trust — something natural, dependable, and kind to the planet. If it makes your day a little fresher and your routine a little better, we've done our job.

Colin
Founder, AGAIN
